# consilium-bench branding

## Name
**consilium-bench** - lowercase, hyphenated, matching the foundry's bench
family (robocorrupt-bench, synsync-bench). "Consilium" is the paper's term
for structured multidisciplinary review; "-bench" says what it is.

## Positioning (one line)
"The open harness the AI Consilium paper didn't ship: role opinions,
interactive consilium, transparent rubric scoring vs the resolved diagnosis."

## Honest-scope tagline (ships in README + Space description)
"Transparent rule-based rubric proxies. The paper's physician-calibrated
judge is not replicated here and is never claimed."

## Visual identity
- Title cards: deep navy `#0b1020` background, steel-blue accent `#1a73e8`,
  light text `#d7e0f5` (see `demo/make_demo_video.py`).
- Badges (README): MIT, Python 3.10+, "CPU only", "$0".
- Social preview text: "consilium-bench: open clinical second-opinion
  benchmark harness. Structured beats freeform, measured honestly."

## Voice
Measured, not marketing. Every claim carries a number from `results/` or is
labeled a proxy. The scope-honesty note is load-bearing: it appears in the
README, the app description, every report footer, and the paper writeup.
Never soften it for launch copy.
